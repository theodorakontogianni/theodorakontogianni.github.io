I am an Associate Professor in 3D Computer Vision at the
[Department of Applied Mathematics and Computer Science](https://www.compute.dtu.dk/)
at the [Technical University of Denmark](https://www.dtu.dk/). I am also affiliated
with the [Pioneer Centre for AI](https://www.aicentre.dk/), where I work on 3D
perception and spatial AI for autonomous systems.

Previously, I was a postdoctoral fellow at the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.eth.ch/)
with [Prof. Dr. Konrad Schindler](https://igp.ethz.ch/personen/person-detail.html?persid=143986)
and [Prof. Dr. Siyu Tang](https://vlg.inf.ethz.ch/team/Prof-Dr-Siyu-Tang.html).
I received my PhD from the [Computer Vision Group](https://www.vision.rwth-aachen.de/)
at [RWTH Aachen University](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/go/id/a/?lidx=1), supervised
by [Prof. Dr. Bastian Leibe](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ZcULDB0AAAAJ).
I also completed two internships at Google Research, in the US and Zurich.

My research interests are in computer vision, with a focus on 3D scene
understanding, interactive learning, and continual learning.
