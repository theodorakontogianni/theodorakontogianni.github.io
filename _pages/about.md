I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the [ETH AI Center](https://ai.ethz.ch/) with
[Prof. Dr. Konrad Schindler](https://igp.ethz.ch/personen/person-detail.html?persid=143986) and
[Prof. Dr. Siyu Tang]("https://vlg.inf.ethz.ch/team/Prof-Dr-Siyu-Tang.html").
		      
Prior to this I received my Ph.D. at the
[Computer Vision Group](https://www.vision.rwth-aachen.de/) with
[Prof. Dr. Bastian Leibe](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=ZcULDB0AAAAJ) at
[RWTH Aachen University](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/go/id/a/?lidx=1).

During my Ph.D I had the opportunity to be a Google Intern, first with [Tobias Weyand](https://research.google/people/tobias-weyand/) in Google LA and then with [Vittorio Ferrari](https://sites.google.com/view/vittoferrari) and [Jasper Uijlings](https://research.google/people/jasper-uijlings/) in Google Research Zurich.
            
My research interests are computer vision with a focus on 3D scene understanding and in particular interactive and continual learning for autonomous agents.
